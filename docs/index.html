FOR WINDOWS 10 AND 11 · OPEN SOURCE · BUILDING IN PUBLIC

The top of your screen, put to work.

Nabbit is a small island that lives at the top of Windows. Save a video from a copied link, drop a file to convert it or file it into a pinned folder, and keep notes in the Tack you host. It stays out of the way until you need it.

A small island at the top of Windows: save videos in one click, convert and file what you drop on it, and keep notes in your own Tack.

DOWNLOADS

Save videos without the flag soup.

Built on yt-dlp and ffmpeg, with the hard parts turned into one tap. Nothing downloads until you say so.

  • Four ways in: copy a link, drag it to the top edge, send it from the browser, or press Ctrl+Alt+DCopy, drag to the top edge, send from the browser, or press Ctrl+Alt+D
  • Presets remembered per site, with trim, chapters, subtitles and playlists one tap awayPresets per site, trim, chapters, subtitles, playlists
  • When YouTube changes, Nabbit says what to do in plain words and updates its enginePlain-language fixes when YouTube changes

NOTES · TACK

Notes that sync with your own Tack.

Point Nabbit at the Tack you host yourself. Your notes stay on your server, and you pick them up anywhere Tack runs.

Capture from the island with #labels and !now, pop notes out as stickies, keep working offline.

  • Capture from the island with #labels, !now and “tomorrow 9am”
  • Pop any note out as a sticky, or pin a board to the desktop
  • Works offline and syncs when you are back. No Tack? Keep notes on this PC only
  • Save any copied link to Tack, and bring your bookmarks over from Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

FILES

File it from the top of the screen.

Pin the folders and files you reach for, then drop anything on the island to move it, convert it or shrink it. Everything runs on your PC, and the original is never replaced.

  • Pins for folders and files. Hover for the newest files, drop to move, or undo if you slip.Pins for folders and files: hover, drop to move, undo
  • Fit a video under Discord’s 20 MB or email’s 25 MB, turn iPhone HEIC into JPG, trim, or make a GIF.Fit under 20 MB, HEIC to JPG, trim, make a GIF
  • Right-click any file and Send to Nabbit. Results land next to the originals.Send to Nabbit from any file’s right-click menu

AND THE REST

Small tools, one place.

Clipboard history

Search everything you copied for 30, 60 or 90 days. Passwords and card numbers are skipped.

30, 60 or 90 days; skips passwords

Selected text, anywhere

Select text in any app and press Ctrl+Alt+S to change case, tidy lines or save it to Tack.

Ctrl+Alt+S to change case, tidy or save to Tack

Now playing

Controls for any app that talks to Windows media, from music to browser tabs.

Any app with Windows media controls

Focus timer

Pomodoro-style rounds as a live pill, optionally linked to a Tack card.

Rounds as a live pill

Mic and camera dot

See which app is listening or watching, the moment it starts.

See who is listening or watching

Every browser’s bookmarks

Import Chrome, Edge and Firefox bookmarks into Tack. Folders become labels.

Chrome, Edge and Firefox into Tack

YOUR SETUP

Island, tray or widgets.

Same tools, three places to keep them. Switch any time in Settings.

Island + tray

A live pill at the top and the full window in the tray. The default.

Tray only

No overlay. Progress on the tray icon and in Windows notifications.

Widgets

Sticky notes and a pinned board on your desktop, synced with Tack.
